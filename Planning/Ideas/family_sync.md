# FamilySync

Family schedules can be hard to manage and compete for time, So an app or website can use AI to take written, messy messages into structured schedules to help manage time

## Details

### Problem

Family activities compete for time, transport, and shared responsibilities.

### Solution

Create structured schedules that are easily accessible to manage family time.

### Prototype

A simple website, type a quick message, and get that converted into a timetable

```
Doctor's appointment at 3
```

And it gets added into a timetable!

### AI Contribution

Convert quick and messy messages into structured schedules.
